Multi-hop selection works when the reader is inside the loop

Takeaways

  • Plain PPO saturates at ~28 F1: it selects blind and aggregates by max-confidence
  • Bridge feedback + learned COMMIT: 37.5 F1 (+9.5), holds on unseen tables and readers
  • Action dropout and RLOO help; self-imitation and privileged critics hurt
  • Vanilla GRPO collapses on sparse multi-hop rewards; Dr. GRPO / RLOO fix it

Full results, significance tests and limitations: report and results/loop/REPORT.md · 8 / 8

To conclude. Plain PPO entity selection plateaus around 28 F1 because it chooses each hop without seeing what the reader found, and leaves the final answer to the reader's maximum confidence. Putting the reader inside the loop, with bridge feedback in the state and a learned COMMIT action, reaches 37.5 F1 with the same training budget, a gain of 9.5 points that holds on unseen tables and with an unseen reader. From the literature, action dropout and the critic-free RLOO help, while self-imitation and a privileged critic hurt, and vanilla GRPO collapses under sparse multi-hop rewards unless its standard-deviation normalisation is removed. Next we will train RiL-PPO with action dropout for longer with five seeds, extend to a horizon of four and cell-level nodes, put an LLM reader in the loop, and, as a stretch goal, tackle multi-table joins on MMQA.
