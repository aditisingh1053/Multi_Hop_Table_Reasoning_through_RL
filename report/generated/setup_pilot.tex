Three settings were changed once, after a pilot run and before any reported experiment. The pilot (PPO, seed~0, on the laptop CPU) used 512 episodes per iteration, a critic learning rate of $5\times10^{-4}$ and three critic-only iterations. After ten iterations its validation F1 had not improved on the cloned policy (23.4 at the start, 22.9 after five iterations, 22.0 after ten), and the critic's explained variance stayed below 0.2. A critic that explains so little of the return turns the GAE advantages into little more than centred returns. We therefore doubled the number of episodes per iteration for all three optimisers, doubled the critic learning rate and extended the critic warm-up to five iterations. The pilot used only training and validation data.
