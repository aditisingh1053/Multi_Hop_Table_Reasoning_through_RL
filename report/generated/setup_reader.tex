Reading one (question, entity) pair takes about 120~ms on our CPU when windows are batched by length. Outputs are reproducible: on 300 sampled pairs the answers were identical across thread counts and batch compositions, and scores differed by at most $1.1\times10^{-5}$. We also tried dynamic int8 quantisation of the reader; it was at most about 7\% faster on this CPU and changed the answer for 48\% of 200 sampled pairs, so all experiments use the full-precision model.
